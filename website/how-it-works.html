How it works

Keeps the server's Eagler channels alive

Pre-1.13 servers use channel names like EAG|Skins-1.8, which a 1.13+ client cannot accept. Wyrm maps them to eag: names and sends replies back under the server's spelling.

Channel bridge

Maps Eagler channel names across the 1.12.2 / 1.13 step and restores the server's spelling on replies.

Works with Wyvern

Wyvern covers older clients on newer servers; ViaRewind (copy included) covers 1.8.

Own identity

Will get its own package, ids and jar name so it does not clash with upstream.
